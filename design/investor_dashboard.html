PART 05

Value Proposition

For remote adult-child caregivers coordinating a parent's chronic condition who have no reliable way to know if medication was taken or is about to run out, FamCare is a family health-coordination app that turns one person's invisible, unpaid tracking work into a shared, predictive system — unlike generic reminder apps or human-staffed concierge services, because it is the only self-serve software product built around India-specific refill logistics and multi-caregiver visibility, at a fraction of concierge pricing.

Functional

Fewer missed doses, fewer refill gaps, one shared record instead of paper/memory.

Emotional

Peace of mind without guilt-inducing daily check-in calls.

Economic

₹149/mo vs. the cost of a missed-dose event or concierge pricing.

Strategic

Every logged dose compounds into the Phase-3 data moat.

PART 08

MVP — Redesigned

Notice → remind → log → predict refill → share visibility. Nothing else, until this loop is proven.

P0 — Must-have to launch

  • OCR medication profile capture
  • Dose reminders (rule-based)
  • Mark taken/skipped (1-tap)
  • Refill prediction & alert (consumption-based, not calendar)
  • Family/shared account linking
  • Remote caregiver status view
  • Low-friction onboarding (<90 sec to first reminder)
"The biggest pain point is not knowing whether to take tablets before or after food, and having to carry medicines around."— Ganesh, verbatim interview

Explicitly excluded (deferred with intent)

  • Symptom/clinical chatbot — liability risk, needs legal sign-off before scoping
  • Pharmacy auto-refill/ordering — needs flat-fee licensing partnerships first
  • Drug interaction checker — needs a licensed drug database, not model knowledge
  • Voice input / NLP search / telemedicine booking — reasonable v2/v3, none load-bearing for the core hypothesis

Validation gates

10-question interviews → waitlist (target 11%+ visitor→signup) → usability testing (5 users/round, 78%+ task success, SUS 68+) → Sean Ellis PMF question (target 40%+ "very disappointed")

MVP user journey & the Aha Moment

Sign-up → OCR onboarding → Mark dose taken/skipped → Aha moment: first correctly-predicted refill alert fires before the family would have noticed on their own → Repeat daily logging → Conversion (adding member #2 = the paywall).
